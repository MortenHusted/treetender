# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss the behavior and evidence, explain disagreements, and make room for different experience levels. Human and AI-assisted contributions are held to the same standards.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing and deliberate disclosure of private information are not acceptable. Do not use issues or pull requests for spam or personal attacks.

This applies to project issues, pull requests and other project spaces. The maintainer may remove content, restrict participation or block accounts to protect the community. Serious or repeated violations may lead to a permanent restriction.

Report abusive content through GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** controls rather than repeating private details publicly. For security vulnerabilities, use the separate private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). GitHub's [community guidelines](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines) also apply.
